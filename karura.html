Why the Karura Run?

The Karura Youth Run is the official Nairobi activation for the Proposed Hekima University Molo Marathon. It brings together the energy of the city's youth with the tranquility of Karura Forest.

  • ✓ Promote High Quality Education
  • ✓ Leadership & Social Transformation
  • ✓ Networking & Community Building
  • ✓ Health & Wellness through Sport
