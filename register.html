1. Event Overview

The Proposed Hekima University Molo Marathon 2026 takes place on 8 August 2026 in Molo, Nakuru County, Kenya. It is organised by the Proposed Hekima University and Global Link Marketing in partnership with Athletics Kenya (AK), KPRA, Kenya Red Cross, Jesuit Eastern Africa, and Nakuru County Government.

2. Participation Requirements

  • Each participant must complete the entry form fully and accurately.
  • Registration is only confirmed upon receipt of the full KES 500 entry fee.
  • Participants must be physically capable of completing their chosen race distance.
  • The 21KM Half Marathon is open to participants aged 18+ on race day.
  • Participants under 18 must be accompanied by a responsible adult and provide written parental/guardian consent.

3. Registration, Fees & BIB Numbers

  • Entry fee is KES 500 per person (all categories).
  • Entry fees are non-refundable unless the event is cancelled by the Organiser.
  • BIB numbers are issued upon payment verification and sent to your registered email.
  • BIB numbers are non-transferable. Ranges: 21KM (11xxx M / 12xxx F); 10KM (21xxx M / 22xxx F); Corporate (31xxx M / 32xxx F); Community (41xxx M / 42xxx F); 5KM (51xxx M / 52xxx F).

4. Race Day Rules & Conduct

  • Participants must adhere to the official race route. Deviation may result in disqualification.
  • Headphones are discouraged for safety.
  • Comply with all instructions from marshals, medical personnel, and officials.
  • Aggressive or unsporting behaviour may result in disqualification and removal.
  • Cut-off times: 21KM — 3 hours; 10KM — 90 minutes; 5KM — open (no cut-off).

5. Safety, Medical & Liability Waiver

Participants acknowledge that running is a strenuous physical activity that may result in injury, illness, or in extreme cases, death. The Organiser, its partners, sponsors, and agents shall not be held liable for any injury, illness, loss, or equipment damage incurred during participation. Participants participate entirely at their own risk.

6. Image Rights & Data Privacy

By registering, participants consent to the use of their images and likeness captured during the Event for promotional purposes. Personal data will not be shared with third parties without consent, except where required by law or for event administration.

7. Refunds & Cancellations

Entry fees are non-refundable if a participant withdraws. In the event of cancellation by the Organiser due to force majeure, participants will be offered a deferral or partial credit. No cash refunds will be issued.

8. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Kenyan courts.
